Privacy Policy

Instant Mechanic · Effective 2 November 2026

Instant Mechanic ("the app", "we", "us") helps you find garages and mechanics near you and send them a service request. The app is developed by Disha Gangwar, an individual developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect what is needed to run your account and pass your request to the garage you choose. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your information. You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password, to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider; we cannot see them.
  • Full name and phone number, which you enter when signing up and which are used to fill in your service requests.

Service requests and reviews

When you request a service, we store the details you enter and the request's progress:

  • your name and phone number,
  • your vehicle number,
  • a description of the problem,
  • the garage and service you chose,
  • the status of the request, the estimated arrival time and any note the garage adds.

If you rate a garage after a job, we store your rating and any comment.

Location

  • If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to search for garages near you. Android lets you choose precise or approximate location; the app works with either, and you can always type an area or pincode instead.
  • We save the last location you searched from (its coordinates and place name) to your account, so the app can open there next time.
  • We do not track your location in the background. Location is only read while you are using the search.

Garage owners

If you claim a garage listing to manage it, your account is linked to that listing so that you can see and respond to the requests sent to it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, payment details, advertising ID or browsing activity. When you tap Call, the app simply opens your phone's dialer; we do not record or log calls.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show garages near the location you choose.
  • To send your service request to the garage you picked, and show you its progress.
  • To let garage owners manage the requests sent to their garage.
  • To show garage ratings built from customer reviews.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

3. Who we share it with

The garage you book

When you send a request, the garage that manages that listing in the app can see your name, phone number, vehicle number and problem description, so that they can contact you and do the job. Garages cannot see your email address, your location or requests you sent to other garages.

Service providers that run the app

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system and server functions. Your account and request data is stored there.
  • Google Play services provides your device's location, and Android's built-in place search (which uses Google's geocoding) turns typed place names and coordinates into locations.
  • Map data providers (OpenStreetMap, and where configured Ola Maps or Google Maps Platform) are used by our server to find garages in an area. Only the coordinates and radius of the area being searched are sent, never your name, email or any other account information.

These providers process information only to provide their service to us.

Other sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may disclose it only if required by law, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

4. How we protect it

All data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. Database access rules ensure each person can only read and change their own data, and garages only the requests sent to them. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your account and request history for as long as your account exists, so that you can see your past requests.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the menu on the home screen and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your profile, your saved location, all your service requests and your reviews. If you manage a garage, it is released, but the public garage listing itself stays in the app.

If you no longer have the app installed, see how to request account deletion.

Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's routine backups for a short period before those backups are overwritten.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can deny or revoke location permission in your phone's settings at any time and search by typing an area instead.
  • Access and correction: you can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account in the app, or ask us to do it.

To make a request, or if you have a concern about how your information is handled, email us at helpinstant72@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Instant Mechanic is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how your information is used, we will tell you in the app.
