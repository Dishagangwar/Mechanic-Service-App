Delete your account

Instant Mechanic, by Disha Gangwar

In the app

The quickest way, and it takes effect immediately:

  • Open Instant Mechanic and sign in.
  • On the home screen, tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  • Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email helpinstant72@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Instant Mechanic account". We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and sign-in details.
  • Your profile: name, phone number and saved search location.
  • All your service requests, including vehicle numbers and problem descriptions.
  • Your ratings and reviews of garages.

What is kept

  • Public garage listings stay in the app. If you managed a garage, it is released and no longer linked to you.

Nothing about you is kept after deletion, apart from routine hosting backups, which are overwritten after a short period.
